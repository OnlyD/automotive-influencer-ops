# AI workflows

Implemented workflows must have a versioned manifest, schemas, templates, prompt, fictional examples, and tests. The local AI Runner registers only approved workflow ID/version pairs. Arbitrary prompts and commands are not supported. Presenter-facing scripts are rendered as Spanish Word drafts; structured JSON stays internal for validation and future indexing. Detailed and promotional scripts consume source-linked vehicle research and select a small, relevant subset of facts. Promotional scripts keep that research separate from presenter-supplied offer terms, mark only missing or explicitly uncertain commercial details, and always require commercial review. Each output remains subject to the approval gate declared in its manifest.
