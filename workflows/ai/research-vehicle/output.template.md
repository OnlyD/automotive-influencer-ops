# Research bundle

- Workflow: `research-vehicle@1.1.0`
- Vehicle: `veh_example`
- Candidate facts: list each candidate with its exact field, value, scope, and source IDs. Candidate facts are unverified.
- Sources: preserve source ID, title, publisher, URL, retrieval time, locale, and reliability tier.
- Open questions: record missing evidence, applicability gaps, or conflicting sources.
- Warnings: identify time-sensitive or market/trim limitations.

The output must validate against `output.schema.json`; no candidate is verified by this workflow.
