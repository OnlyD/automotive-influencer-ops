# Promotional script draft

Version `1.3.0` creates a Spanish sales-focused video draft from source-linked vehicle research prepared earlier in the same presenter conversation and optional same-day offer terms. It uses only two or three attractive vehicle facts relevant to the audience and promo angle, not the complete research. If fewer than two usable facts are available, it uses supported facts and adds a warning. The presenter may supply same-day offer terms in natural language; the workflow can use them verbatim as unverified draft input and must not imply that it independently confirmed them. Only missing or explicitly uncertain price, promotion, availability, financing, or eligibility fields receive spoken placeholders. Validity is stated in both speech and on-screen copy. The closing CTA invites contact (with a placeholder when no method was supplied) and following, liking, and commenting. The presenter receives a four-column Word document; structured JSON stays internal. Every result requires commercial review and is non-publishable.
