# automotive-presenter

The influencer-facing plugin. All user-facing questions, instructions, errors, explanations, and generated content must always be in Spanish. Technical implementation instructions remain in English.

The `actualizar-influencer` skill refreshes this plugin, starts the presenter-only Spanish context, and can save new Spanish defect reports under `docs/feedback/influencer/` after presenter review. The two script skills are `generar-guion` for detailed reviews based on the operator's research handoff, using two or three relevant research facts, and `generar-guion-promocional` for sales drafts combining two or three attractive vehicle facts with separate offer terms supplied by the presenter. Both require operator-provided vehicle research, produce a Spanish Word document with timed scenes, short visual directions, spoken narration, and on-screen text, and keep structured JSON and schemas internal. Only genuinely missing or explicitly uncertain commercial terms receive placeholders. All drafts still require factual or commercial review and are not published by the skill. `actualizar-herramientas` remains as a compatibility entry that routes to the supported skill.
