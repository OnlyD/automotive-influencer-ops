# automotive-presenter

The influencer-facing plugin. All user-facing questions, instructions, errors, explanations, and generated content must always be in Spanish. Technical implementation instructions remain in English.

The `actualizar-influencer` skill refreshes this plugin, starts the presenter-only Spanish context, and can save new Spanish defect reports under `docs/feedback/influencer/` after presenter review. The standard presenter flow is `actualizar-influencer`, then `investigar-vehiculo` in the same conversation, then either `generar-guion-promocional` (priority) or `generar-guion` (detailed review). Research and script writing stay separate. Both script skills select two or three source-linked vehicle facts, include contact and follow/like/comment CTAs, and produce a Spanish Word document with timed scenes, concise visuals, narration, and on-screen text. Missing commercial terms and missing contact methods get clear placeholders. Internal JSON and schemas are never presented. All drafts still require factual or commercial review and are not published by the skill. `actualizar-herramientas` remains as a compatibility entry that routes to the supported skill.
