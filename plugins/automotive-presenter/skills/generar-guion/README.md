# Generar guion

This Spanish-language skill consumes a source-linked research bundle prepared earlier in the same conversation and creates a detailed vehicle-review draft as a timed, four-column Word document. It uses two or three research facts relevant to the audience and angle, linked to their sources, without turning the script into a spec sheet. Facts remain candidate claims pending technical review. Its closing includes contact and follow/like/comment calls to action. JSON and schemas remain internal.
