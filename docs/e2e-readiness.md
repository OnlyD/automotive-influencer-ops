# Full-trial readiness and resumption

## Current boundary

The supervised **local** machinery can prepare either a promotional video or a separate-voice-over video and produce an approved manual publication package. Contract/component checks and complete synthetic promotional and voice-over tests validate integration through all states, including retries, reviewed media, manual packages, fictional receipts and metrics. The full real production trial and any publication remain unrun.

Do not describe this as deployed AWS, authenticated remote presenter operation or automatic social publishing. The pilot deliberately uses the manual handoff allowed by specification section 39.4. Automated/API publishing remains contingent on a separately chosen provider and platform permissions/audits.

Inventory XLSX support, an unattended publisher, synthetic voice generation and fully automatic editing are not provided by this local path. XLSX remains a later importer increment; the tested inventory input is CSV.

## Information needed for the trial

1. Choose promotional or voice-over content and the exact vehicle/year/trim/market.
2. Retrieve/review relevant sources; use two or three facts in the script. Supply the actual intended contact method.
3. For promotional content, confirm terms, eligibility, availability, validity and commercial disclosure. Missing terms can remain in an early draft but block recording/preparation.
4. Provide original visual media and the presenter's audio, rights/consent evidence and approved scene timing. Watch/listen rather than relying only on format validation.
5. Establish the intended platform/account and current publishing requirements. Account creation alone does not grant a direct-post API permission.
6. Configure an operator-managed off-device backup before irreplaceable originals are ingested. Preserve ignored local state across clones/machines.
7. Explicitly authorize the trial and, separately, its final publication.

## Exact resumption point

Register one production with `pnpm operations -- create`, import the source-linked draft or register its normalized candidate, then verify the selected canonical fact/source records, bind their hashes with `bind-facts`, then record factual/creative/applicable commercial reviews. Follow `local-production.md` from the shooting plan through media, edit, master reviews and the platform package. Run the worker explicitly; do not activate n8n polling before reviewing its queued work.

At the publication gate, use the approved manual handoff on the intended account, then retain the attested remote ID/URL/time. Do not mark publication from export, a local schedule record or a fabricated receipt.

If the trial must run across independent presenter/operator machines with always-available state, stop that remote setup at increment F and resolve AWS region/account, identity, retention and backup configuration first. Those choices do not block the local supervised trial.

See [the self-audit](self-audit.md) and [validation record](validation-report.md) for findings, evidence and remaining limits. Synthetic 20-second tones/images do not establish real speech quality, presenter usability or the 120–150-second pilot’s performance.
