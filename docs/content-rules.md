# Content rules

- All presenter interaction and audience copy is Spanish. Technical instructions, code and operator interfaces are English.
- Research candidates require human factual review for the exact vehicle/year/trim/market. Retain source links and dates; do not verify facts merely by schema validation.
- Scripts use the relevant two or three research facts, especially concise attractive highlights for promotional content. Commercial terms have a separate review lifecycle.
- Every presenter script closes with contact and follow/like/comment invitations. Keep an unsupplied contact destination visibly pending; never invent contacts or account handles.
- Promotional drafts may contain missing-data placeholders. Recording/rendering requires resolving them, confirming eligibility and stating validity in speech and on-screen copy.
- Creative changes preserve locked metadata and produce a new version. Metadata hashes cannot determine whether paraphrased text changed meaning; operator review remains required.
- Review actual voice, subtitles, framing, timing and audio against the approved script. Technical format checks do not establish factual or editorial quality.
- Record music/image/filming origins, license or consent evidence and human rights review. No platform music license is presumed transferable.
- Clips need complete context, an appropriate closing and their own human reviews. A four-to-six target never overrides quality.
- Use platform-specific captions/disclosures, explicit intended accounts and exact-version publication approvals. Manual handoff is the pilot method. No export or local scheduling record constitutes publication.
