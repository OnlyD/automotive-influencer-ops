# ADR-0011: All Presenter Scripts Include Contact and Engagement CTAs

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-27

## Context

Presenter scripts need a useful closing that tells viewers how to respond. A script that omits contact and social engagement lines creates additional editing work, while invented contact details or account handles would be misleading.

## Decision

- Every promotional and detailed presenter script includes spoken closing copy inviting viewers to get in touch and separately to follow the account, like the video, and comment.
- Use a contact method or account handle only when the presenter supplies it. When no contact method is provided, include `[MEDIO DE CONTACTO POR CONFIRMAR]` in the spoken contact call.
- Do not invent phone numbers, handles, links, or dealership contacts.
- The local runner validates that the closing includes a contact invitation, the supplied contact method or fallback placeholder, and follow/like/comment language.
- Commercial offer validity and its placeholder rules remain separate and apply only to promotional scripts.

## Consequences

- Both presenter Word templates include the closing calls in the final spoken narration.
- A missing contact method remains visibly editable before recording.
- Exact brand voice, contact destination, and social account details remain configurable when supplied; none are embedded as real project data.
